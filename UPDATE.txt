CRIMSON PC CHECK V2 - UPDATE FROM SENTINEL

1. Stop your OLD server window with Ctrl+C.
2. Extract this ZIP into a new folder.
3. Copy the contents of crimson-pc-check into your EXISTING sentinel folder,
   replacing application files. Do NOT delete or replace the hidden .sentinel
   folder: it contains your password configuration, saved library and reports.
4. In that existing folder, double-click start-crimson.cmd.
5. Refresh http://localhost:3000 in your browser. Sign in again if needed.

Your old reports stay available. No personal state is included in this ZIP.
Do not upload .sentinel to GitHub or share it.

For a fresh installation, use QUICKSTART.txt.
Old start-sentinel.cmd remains a compatibility shortcut.
Use the new crimson-pc-check-windows-amd64.exe or the Guided scanner button,
not an old copy of sentinel-scanner-windows-amd64.exe.
